Step by Step
The Complete Counselling Process

JoSAA 2026 is entirely online. Follow these 8 steps in order.

🔐
Step 01

Register on JoSAA

Visit josaa.nic.in. Log in with JEE Main / Advanced roll number + date of birth. Fill the basic registration form carefully.

📝
Step 02

Fill & Lock Choices

Add institute + branch combos in preference order. Modify unlimited times. The system auto-locks if you don't lock manually by deadline.

🔍
Step 03

Check Mock Allotments

Two mock rounds show tentative results. No fee needed. Use these to reorder your preferences before the final lock.

📊
Step 04

Check Allotment Result

Log into JoSAA portal after each round. No emails/SMS — you must check yourself. Download your provisional allotment letter.

💳
Step 05

Pay Seat Acceptance Fee

₹35,000 (Gen/OBC/EWS) or ₹15,000 (SC/ST/PwD). Pay within the round deadline to stay in the process. Refundable minus ₹3,000.

🔄
Step 06

Float / Slide / Freeze

Float = try for better college or branch. Slide = upgrade branch in same college. Freeze = keep current seat permanently.

📤
Step 07

Online Document Reporting

Upload self-attested scans of all required documents to the JoSAA portal. Do this within the reporting window of your round.

🏛️
Step 08

Physical Reporting at IITB

Carry originals to IIT Bombay for verification. Only after successful physical reporting is your admission confirmed.

Who Can Apply
Eligibility Criteria

You must satisfy all criteria simultaneously. IIT and NIT+ system have different requirements.

🏛️ For IITs (incl. IIT Bombay)

  • Valid JEE Advanced 2026 rank (must have appeared in both papers)
  • Also need valid JEE Main 2026 score for registration
  • Class 12 with minimum 5 subjects
  • Minimum 75% marks in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST/PwD)
  • OR top 20 percentile of your board in Class 12
  • Must be born on or after Oct 1, 2001 (Gen/OBC/EWS) or Oct 1, 1996 (SC/ST/PwD)
  • Max 2 attempts at JEE Advanced

🏫 For NITs / IIITs / GFTIs

  • Valid JEE Main 2026 rank/score only (no JEE Advanced needed)
  • Class 12 with Physics + Math as compulsory subjects
  • Minimum 75% marks in Class 12 (65% for SC/ST/PwD)
  • OR top 20 percentile of your board
  • NRI / OCI candidates eligible with JEE Main rank
  • Home state quota applies for NITs
📌 Note: Qualifying JEE Advanced is a prerequisite for IIT Bombay. Your 12th board percentage is verified during physical reporting — ensure you meet it.

Strategy Guide
7 Essential Tips for IITB Aspirants

Straight-talking advice from the JoSAA process — avoid the most common mistakes.

1
Order choices as if each is your final option The system gives you the best seat from your list that your rank qualifies for. If IITB CSE is #1 and IITB EE is #2, and you get CSE — great. But if IITB is your only goal, don't add lower-ranked IITs unless you're genuinely okay with them.
2
Use Float/Slide aggressively in early rounds Keep Float or Slide active in Rounds 1–4. The system automatically upgrades you within your locked choice list. Freeze only when you're genuinely happy — or it's Round 5/6.
3
Never miss the fee-payment window You typically have 24–48 hours to pay after a result. Miss it once = out of the entire process. Set 3 alarms: at result, 12 hrs later, and 2 hrs before deadline.
4
Use category rank, not CRL, if you're from a reserved category JoSAA allots reserved category seats using your category-specific rank. Your CRL may be 5,000 but if your OBC rank is 1,800, that's what matters for OBC seats at IITB.
5
Female candidates: check supernumerary seats separately IITs add ~14% extra seats for female candidates over sanctioned intake. These have a separate (usually higher numerical rank) cutoff — often 15–30% more relaxed than gender-neutral closing ranks.
6
Prepare documents before Day 1 of registration OBC-NCL certificate validity, EWS financial year, 12th board marks — verify all of these now. Document issues have caused admission cancellations even after fee payment.
7
Don't withdraw just because you didn't get your top choice in Round 1 Round 6 closing ranks are always lower (more candidates) than Round 1. Staying in the process and using Float often yields significant upgrades. Patience pays.
Frequently Asked
FAQ — JoSAA & IIT Bombay

Answers to the questions that come up every year.

Float means you want to upgrade to any better option from your choice list — different branch OR different institute. Slide restricts upgrades to a better branch within your currently allotted institute only. Freeze means you're satisfied and don't want any change — your current allotment becomes final. Choose wisely: Freeze is irreversible within that round.
Almost never withdraw early. Keep Float active. Seats open up as other candidates upgrade, withdraw, or don't pay fees. Round 6 sees many upgrades relative to Round 1. If you get a seat you don't want, you can always withdraw after the final round with a partial refund (minus ₹3,000).
No. Once the choice-filling window closes (or you manually lock), no changes are possible. This is why the mock allotment rounds are critical — use them to reorder your preferences. The system auto-locks your last saved order if you don't manually lock.
Yes, partially. If you withdraw before the end of the final round (Round 6), you get the fee back minus ₹3,000 processing charge. If you don't report to IITB physically or withdraw after Round 6, the full amount is forfeited. The ₹3,000 processing fee is non-refundable in all cases.
AAT is a separate aptitude test for admission to B.Arch programmes at IITs (Bombay, Kharagpur, Roorkee, Varanasi, Guwahati). To apply for AAT, you must first qualify JEE Advanced and register for it separately during the JoSAA window. AAT results are used alongside your JEE Advanced rank for B.Arch seat allocation. IIT Bombay offers B.Arch through this route.
IITs add approximately 14% extra seats over the sanctioned intake specifically for female candidates. These appear as a separate pool in JoSAA choice filling. The closing ranks for female supernumerary seats are significantly more relaxed — typically 3x to 5x higher than gender-neutral closing ranks (e.g., if CSE Gender-Neutral closes at ~65, the Female pool may close around 400–500). This means female candidates with a much higher AIR/category rank can still get their preferred branch. Always enter your rank in the Female Supernumerary pool in our predictor for accurate results.
IITB verifies originals of: Class 10 certificate (DOB), Class 12 marksheet + certificate (board percentage), JEE Advanced admit card + scorecard, category certificate (if applicable), PwD certificate (if applicable), and your JoSAA allotment letter. Any discrepancy or missing document can result in admission cancellation.
You can withdraw from JoSAA at any point. If you withdraw before Round 6 ends, you get a partial refund (minus ₹3,000). You are then free to apply to private colleges or state counselling. Note: Once you withdraw from JoSAA, you cannot re-enter that year's JoSAA process.
No. IIT admissions are based exclusively on JEE Advanced All India Rank (AIR). JEE Main rank is only needed to be eligible to appear for JEE Advanced. For NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs, JEE Main rank is what matters.
CSAB (Central Seat Allocation Board) conducts special rounds after JoSAA ends, for vacant seats in NITs, IIITs, and GFTIs only — not IITs. If you're targeting an NIT and didn't get your preferred branch/college through JoSAA, CSAB gives you another shot. You must explicitly opt-in for CSAB during the JoSAA process. Check csab.nic.in for details.
